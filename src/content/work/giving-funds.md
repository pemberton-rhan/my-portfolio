Fewer than one percent of Americans use a donor-advised fund. They usually come with fees, high minimums and paperwork. GoFundMe set out to change that with Giving Funds: no fees, no minimum balance, and grants to any of 1.4 million charities.

The page had to explain an unfamiliar financial product in plain language, answer the tax questions up front, and move people to open a fund, on GoFundMe’s WordPress platform and on the product’s launch date.
