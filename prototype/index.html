About

A designer’s eye, an engineer’s habits, a lead’s patience.

I came to the web through design. After serving as a hospital corpsman in the US Navy, I worked as a graphic and web designer before moving into WordPress engineering full time in 2013.

Since then I’ve built and run sites for law firms, agencies and GoFundMe, and spent the last several years leading the engineers who build them: setting standards, reviewing code, and turning good habits into process that a whole team can follow.
